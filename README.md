# AutoResQ – AI-Powered Emergency Response & Road Safety Platform

## Saving Lives Through Intelligent Emergency Response

AutoResQ is an AI-powered road safety and emergency response platform developed to reduce rescue delays and improve emergency coordination during road accidents. The platform combines intelligent reporting, live location tracking, emergency communication, cloud-based evidence collection, and AI-assisted workflows to ensure that help reaches victims faster when every second matters.

Designed with a user-centric approach, AutoResQ empowers accident victims, bystanders, emergency contacts, and responders through a seamless ecosystem that enables rapid reporting, secure evidence management, and real-time situational awareness.

---

# Problem Statement

Road accidents remain one of the leading causes of injuries and fatalities worldwide. In many situations, victims do not receive immediate assistance due to:

* Delayed accident reporting
* Lack of accurate location information
* Inability to quickly contact emergency responders
* Insufficient evidence collection
* Poor coordination between victims, bystanders, and emergency services

These challenges often result in critical delays during the golden hour, reducing the effectiveness of emergency medical intervention.

---

# Solution

AutoResQ provides an integrated emergency response platform that allows users to instantly report accidents, activate SOS alerts, share real-time location data, notify emergency contacts, and collect digital evidence.

The platform leverages modern mobile technologies, cloud infrastructure, geolocation services, and AI-powered assistance to create a faster, smarter, and more reliable emergency response system.
